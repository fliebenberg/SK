## How to use this guide

Same rules as the [admin guide](admin-user.md): a guide, not a script; concrete over general;
follow the emotion; nothing about ScoreKeeper until §12.

**Establish coach or manager first (§1.3).** These are often two people with two very different
jobs — one picks the team, the other chases the permission slips — and the app treats them as
separate roles. If your participant is both, ask them to separate the two hats as they talk. If
they are only one, ask who does the other half and what passes between them.

**The unit here is the week and the team**, not the season and the event. Where the admin guide
asks "how did you build a draw", this one asks "how did you find out about the fixture". Coaches
are usually on the *receiving* end of the admin's output, and the handover between the two is where
most of the interesting failures live.

**Time budget** (~45–60 min): §1–3, 10 min. §4–7 the core, 25 min. §8–11, 15 min. §12–13, 5 min.
**If you are running short, protect §4 and §5.**

**Coaches often double as scorers.** We are not running a separate scorer interview in round one,
so if this participant scores their own matches, §6.5–6.9 is your chance to capture it — spend the
time there rather than rushing on.

**Referencing:** questions are cited as *section.question* — `4.3` is the third question in §4.
Every list item is written as `1.` in the source; the renderer numbers them. Add new questions at
the **end** of a section to keep existing references stable. Indented bullets are probes, not
questions to read aloud.

## 1. Background

1. What is your name?
1. What organisation and team are you involved with?
1. Are you the coach, the team manager, or both?
   - If both — which half takes more of your time?
   - If one — who does the other half, and how do the two of you divide it?
1. Is this paid, part of another job, or voluntary?
1. How long have you been doing it, and how did you end up with this team?
1. Do you have any other connection to the organisation?
   - Parent of a player, former player, teacher, club member?
1. Are you involved with more than one team, or more than one sport?

## 2. Your team

1. Tell me about the team — sport, age group, level.
1. How many players are in the squad, and how many can play at once?
1. How did the squad get picked in the first place, and by whom?
1. How much does the squad change over a season?
   - Injuries, players joining late, players quitting, players moving up or down a team?
1. Do any of your players also play for another team here?
   - How is that handled when both teams play on the same day?
1. Does anyone play up or down an age group?
1. Who else is around the team — assistant coach, physio, parent helpers, a captain who
   organises things?

## 3. A week in season

1. Walk me through a normal week during season — what happens on which day?
1. When do you train, and how do players know?
1. How do you know what is coming up in the week ahead?
1. What is the first thing you check on a match day morning?
1. How much of your week goes on the organising rather than the coaching?

## 4. Squad, availability and selection *(core)*

1. For your last match — how did you decide who was playing?
1. How do you find out who is available?
   - Do players tell you, or do you have to chase them?
   - How far ahead do you know?
1. What is your record of that — where does the team list actually exist?
1. When does the team get picked relative to the match?
1. How do players find out they are in or not in the team?
1. What happens when someone drops out late?
   - How late has it been? What did you do?
1. Does anyone else need to approve or see the selection before it goes out?
   - A head of sport, a parent, a league eligibility rule?
1. Do you have to record who actually turned up, as opposed to who was picked?
   - For training as well as matches?
   - Does anyone ever ask you for that?
1. Are there rules about who *may* play — registration, minimum game time, age or eligibility
   limits?
   - Who checks that, and what happens when it is got wrong?
1. Do you keep track of how much game time each player is getting across the season?

## 5. Getting information from the organisation *(core)*

1. How do you find out about fixtures — what arrives, from whom, in what form?
1. How far in advance do you normally get that?
1. Where do you then keep it?
1. What happens when a fixture changes — time, venue, opposition, cancellation?
   - How do you find out? How quickly?
   - Tell me about the last time that happened.
1. Have you ever turned up somewhere wrong, or at the wrong time?
1. What do you have to pass on to your players and parents once you have it?
   - How much re-typing is involved?
1. What information do you need that you routinely have to go and ask someone for?
1. What do *you* have to send back up to the organisation, and when?
   - Team sheets, results, availability, attendance?
   - What happens if you are late with it?

## 6. Match day

1. Walk me through the last match — from arriving to leaving.
1. What are you carrying, and what do you have to have with you?
1. Who do you have to deal with when you arrive — opposition coach, official, host?
1. Is a team sheet exchanged or handed in? To whom, in what form?
1. Who keeps the score?
   - Is that you, or someone else? How is it decided?
1. What is actually written down during the game, and on what?
1. Do you record anything beyond the score — scorers, cards, times, substitutions,
   individual performances?
   - What do you use that for afterwards?
1. What is the phone signal like where you play?
1. If you are on the touchline coaching, how realistic is it for you to record anything at the
   same time?
1. What happens if the two sides disagree about the score or an incident?
1. What do you do with whatever you wrote down, once you get home?

## 7. After the match

1. Who needs to know the result, and how fast?
1. How does the result get to them?
1. Does anyone check or confirm it, or is your word final?
1. Do you get to see other teams' results from the same day?
   - Do you want to?
1. Is there anything you have to write up afterwards — a report, a summary, an award?
1. Do you ever look back at earlier results or team lists during the season?
   - What for?
1. At the end of the season, is there anything you have to produce?
   - Awards, colours, player-of-the-season, a report to a head of sport?
   - Where do the numbers for that come from?

## 8. Players, parents and communication

1. Who do you communicate with — the players, the parents, or both?
   - Does that change with the age group?
1. What do you use, and did you choose it or inherit it?
1. What do you send in a normal week?
1. What generates the most back-and-forth or the most chasing?
1. What do parents ask you for that you cannot easily give them?
1. Do parents want to see scores or results while they are not there?
1. Is there anything you deliberately do *not* put in a group chat?
1. How do you handle contact details for players and parents?
   - Where do those live? Who gave them to you?
1. Are there rules about photographs, names or results being shared publicly, especially for
   minors?

## 9. What you keep and where

1. If I asked to see everything you hold about this team, what would you show me and where
   would it be?
1. What is on paper?
1. What is only on your own phone or laptop?
1. What happens to all of it at the end of the season?
1. If you stopped coaching tomorrow, what would the next person get from you, and what would be
   lost?
1. Has anything ever been lost — a team list, a set of results, a phone?

## 10. Tools you use today

1. What apps or systems are you expected to use for this team?
   - Who chose them?
1. Which of them do you actually use, and which do you ignore?
1. What works well enough that you would not want it changed?
1. What have you tried and given up on? Why?
1. Do you ever get given a system by a league or association that you have no say over?
1. What would make you not bother with a new app?
   - Having to log in? Another password? Another notification stream?

## 11. Access and boundaries

1. What are you allowed to change yourself, and what do you have to ask for?
1. What would you *like* to be able to change yourself?
1. Is there anything you would rather not be responsible for?
1. Should you be able to see other teams in your organisation?
   - Their fixtures, their results, their squads?
1. Should the opposition coach be able to see your team sheet? When — before or after?
1. Are there things about your players you would not want other coaches or organisations to see?

## 12. Reactions to the concept *(only after the above)*

> Describe ScoreKeeper briefly and plainly. Then get reactions. Polite interest is not evidence —
> follow every positive with "when did you last have that problem?"

1. Having heard that — where does it fit what you do, and where does it not?
1. What would you need on day one for it to be worth opening at all?
1. *(Selection)* If you could pick your matchday team from your squad in the app, and the
   organisation could see it, would you use that — or is your current way faster?
1. *(Availability)* Would you want players or parents to mark their own availability, or would
   you rather that stayed your job?
1. *(Training and roll call)* Would you use something that let you set up training sessions and
   mark who attended? Or is that admin you do not want?
1. *(Live scores)* Would you want parents watching the score from elsewhere? Is there any downside
   to that at your age group?
1. *(Fixture changes)* If a change to a fixture reached your team automatically, without you
   forwarding it — what would that save you, realistically?
1. Which of those would you actually never touch?
1. Who would have to tell you to use this before you would?

## 13. Wrap-up

1. What should I have asked you that I did not?
1. Could you show me your team list, your group chat, or whatever you use — it would tell me more
   than describing it?
1. Who else should I talk to?
   - A coach at a different level or a different sport? A team manager, if you are a coach?
1. Would you be willing to look at something early and tell us where it is wrong?
1. Can I come back to you with follow-up questions?
